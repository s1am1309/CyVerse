MOODIFY

Understand the expression.

Moodify watches for visible facial-expression patterns — like the corners of a smile, or a furrowed brow — and estimates a Facial Expression Estimate in real time. Every frame is processed locally, inside a small neural model that runs directly in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, recorded, or sent to a server.

Your browser will ask for camera permission. You can stop at any time.
